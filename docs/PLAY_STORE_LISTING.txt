# Awfar CC — Store listing (Google Play)

## Short description
Awfar CC — SIP softphone for business VoIP calls over your PBX.

## Full description
Awfar CC is a business VoIP softphone for agents and teams.

Connect to your company PBX using SIP credentials and make or receive calls over the internet.

Key features:
• SIP login with extension and password
• Outgoing and incoming VoIP calls
• Call history and local contacts
• Presence modes (Online / Do Not Disturb / Offline)
• Tasks and follow-up reminders
• Optional headset / Bluetooth audio support

Who it’s for:
Call center agents and employees who need a mobile softphone connected to their company’s PBX (for example FreePBX / Asterisk-based systems).

Permissions:
The app may request microphone access for calls, contacts for caller ID, phone state for call handling, notifications for incoming calls, and Bluetooth for headsets.

Note:
A valid PBX/SIP account from your organization is required. This app does not provide a phone number by itself.
